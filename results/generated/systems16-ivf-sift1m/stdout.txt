# IVF recall@10 vs QPS Pareto — sift_base

- dim **128**, db **1000000**, queries **200**, nlist **1024**, 2-bit, seed **42**, **20 threads** (rayon, queries fanned out)
- recall@10 is measured against exact cosine top-10 ground truth over the same database; QPS is wall-clock over the full query set.
- trellis is reported in both deployment modes: `trellis_recon` uses a decode-free fp32 reconstruction cache; `trellis_codes` performs per-candidate Viterbi-state decoding from the compact representation.
